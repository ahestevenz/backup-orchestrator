bleiben Backup Orchestrator is made to help people keep their work safe, not to
make money. This page explains in plain language who may use it for free and
who needs a license. The legal terms are in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE): the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0/).
If this page and the license ever seem to disagree, the license decides.

## Free for noncommercial use

You may use, copy and change the software for free for any noncommercial
purpose, under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. That includes:

- personal, hobby and family use, such as backing up your own photos and your
  family's computers;
- education: schools, universities and students;
- charities and other noncommercial organizations;
- research.

## Commercial use needs a license

Using the software for commercial purposes needs a separate commercial license
from bleiben. Commercial use includes:

- a freelance or professional photographer backing up paid work;
- photo studios and other companies.

To ask for a commercial license, contact bleiben through
[www.bleiben.ar](https://www.bleiben.ar).

## Free commercial licenses for small earners

Freelance photographers with less than A$80,000 a year in gross income from
photography receive a commercial license free of charge on request. Above
that, the commercial license is paid; terms on request.

Every license is confirmed in writing by email.

This is a policy of bleiben, not part of the license text, and it may change.
Licenses already granted keep their terms.

## Not allowed without a written agreement

Without a written agreement with bleiben, nobody may:

- sell the software;
- include it in another product;
- offer it as a service.

This protects the code itself, not the idea: anyone may still write a similar
tool from scratch.
